# Revision analysis plan

Recorded 22 September 2026 before fitting the new station and calendar stratified models. This is a revision plan, not a preregistration: the earlier NO2 results have been examined. All model specifications below will be retained in the reported analysis, irrespective of effect direction or interval width.

## Scientific question and estimand

How does monitored surface NO2 differ during sustained compound heat and low wind episodes, compared with other weather regimes in the same local calendar strata, and how much does this contrast vary across the 11 cities? This is a retrospective environmental association study. ERA5 maximum temperature and mean wind define the exposure. Calendar matching, additional weather adjustment, and stable monitor comparisons support a conditional association, not a causal intervention estimate.

The primary outcome is the AQS station daily mean in ppb. Estimate a model separately for each city using monitor by year by month by weekday intercepts. Thus every identifying comparison is within the same monitor, calendar month and year, and weekday. Give each city-day total weight one across available monitors, then report the arithmetic mean of the 11 city estimates. Report all city estimates. The former city-median, 60-day matched change score is a secondary historical comparison and is not the new primary endpoint.

## Data and exposure

Retain the 11 city centres, 25 km monitor inclusion, AQS quality filters, April to October 2019-2024 primary period, and 2019-2022 city-specific 80th temperature and 30th wind percentile thresholds. Use the explicitly selected ERA5 model and consistent local-calendar daily aggregation. Reconcile new extra-variable downloads against the existing frozen temperature/wind series before changing any exposure labels. The 2025 results are a temporal extension, not an untouched validation set.

Primary exposure classes are (i) neither threshold, (ii) heat only, (iii) low wind only, (iv) compound days within retained episodes of at least two consecutive days, and (v) other compound days. Existing seven-day start separation is retained for comparability; unrestricted consecutive runs are a definition sensitivity. Classes are mutually exclusive. The main contrast is episode days versus neither-threshold days; component contrasts compare episode days with heat-only and low-wind-only days. Also estimate the all-compound interaction from a four-regime model; do not label the sustained-episode contrast an interaction.

## Model specification

Fit station calendar stratum fixed effects by weighted within transformation. Calendar-only and weather-adjusted specifications use the same complete data sample. Weather adjustment includes relative humidity, log(1+precipitation), shortwave radiation and wind direction sine/cosine. Each scalar weather covariate uses a natural cubic spline with three degrees of freedom; the direction enters as two circular terms. Transformations and knots use observed weather distributions without NO2. Temperature and wind speed are not included as ordinary nuisance regressors in the primary model because they define the weather regimes. Humidity and radiation adjusted contrasts are conditional associations; both unadjusted-for-weather and adjusted contrasts are reported because these variables may also co-vary with the compound weather system.

No boundary layer height or emissions mechanism is claimed. Residual dependence is addressed by resampling whole city/year contributions, preserving every observation within a sampled block. Use 5000 bootstrap draws, seed 20260922, and report equal-city percentile intervals and a t interval across city estimates as an additional summary. Synchronized-year resampling across sampled cities is a dependence sensitivity. Report leave-one-city and leave-one-year point estimates, rather than choosing the most favorable interval.

## Support and station coverage checks

Report counts of observations, stations, episodes and calendar strata at every processing step. Quantify episode days with at least one neither-threshold observation in their exact station/calendar stratum. A direct-support analysis uses only strata containing both an episode and a neither-threshold day. A fixed core monitor sensitivity selects monitors by at least 80% warm-season availability within city-year, based on missingness rather than NO2 values. Additional city-median models assess the change in measurement aggregation. Missing outcomes are not imputed. Audit historical matched-window station counts, missingness, roster overlap and common-station contrasts.

## Temporal trajectory and robustness

A separate phase model describes days preceding episodes, during episodes, and after episode end (days 1-2 and 3-6). An episode takes precedence over another event's post period; other compound days are modeled separately. Calendar and weather adjustment are the same as in the primary model. This trajectory is descriptive and is not an independent validation of an endpoint derived from it. Report periods 2019-2022, 2023-2024 and 2025 separately without claims of independent confirmation. Also report excluding 2020, equal station rather than equal date weights, unrestricted compound runs, and city-median aggregation. All primary, component and phase contrasts appear together; intervals are pointwise, not multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory tests.

## Decisions and deliverables

### Recorded amendment after numerical rank audit

The first complete run showed that resampled year blocks can lack the rank needed to estimate a contrast, especially in the direct-support sample and short period partitions. The point estimators and every model specification remain unchanged. The already planned cross-city t interval is now the consistently reported pooled interval for **all** models, with 10 degrees of freedom and an explicit approximate-independent-city assumption. It does not remove shared-year dependence or make these purposively selected cities representative of the United States. The original hierarchical and synchronized-year quantiles remain publicly available as **conditional-on-estimability sensitivity summaries**, accompanied by attempted and valid counts, not as unconditional 95% confidence intervals. No unsuccessful repetitions are replaced. For individual-city forest plots, uncertainty is described by a delete-one-year jackknife t interval when at least three years and every deletion contrast are identifiable; otherwise the interval is unavailable. A single year is never assigned a zero-width interval. This amendment was prompted by numerical identifiability, after initial output inspection, and is not portrayed as prospective.

Judge readiness from support, magnitude/precision, city heterogeneity, model stability, coverage sensitivity, source verification and environmental relevance. Positive significance is not a pass criterion. Major unresolved code/data discrepancies preclude a submission-ready claim. The exploration status and observational scope alone do not preclude a paper. Deliver the revised manuscript, reproducible analysis and figure scripts, derived data dictionary and archive package, and a concise issue-resolution report. Public release contains only checked scientific code and derived environmental data, with source attribution and applicable licenses; private correspondence and local secrets are excluded.
